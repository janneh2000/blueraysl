How we work, and why that matters

Mineral wealth only benefits the country it comes from when it is extracted under licence, processed locally where possible, and exported through channels that can be audited. Blue-Ray works with the National Minerals Agency, the Environment Protection Agency Sierra Leone (EPA-SL), local chiefdoms and host communities. We export only through licensed channels and only to vetted buyers.

Our mining operations are backed by the same partners and financiers as the rest of the company — PRAETORIAN Global Group (Sweden), FIRST International Bank Nigeria (SL) Ltd. and IDEFENZ Company Limited (Malaysia) — covering financing, technical capacity and offtake agreements.
