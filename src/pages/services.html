Service 02

Mining & Mineral Export

Blue-Ray's mining division runs seven mineral streams on licensed ground: bauxite, gold, diamonds, heavy mineral concentrate, coltan, construction sand, and the heavy haulage that moves all of it. We handle exploration and extraction through processing and logistics, to export to vetted buyers.

We work with the National Minerals Agency, the Environment Protection Agency Sierra Leone (EPA-SL), local chiefdoms and host communities — because real mineral wealth is the kind that benefits the country it comes from.

End-to-End Mining Capability

01

Geological surveys, sampling, deposit modelling and feasibility studies — to identify what's there and what it's worth.

Open-pit and alluvial extraction supported by our own heavy plant — loaders, excavators and haul trucks.
